BOOFVIZ 0.1.1 adds the clip engine and live performance tools, and makes lyrics and album covers more reliable.

## Install

Download **BOOFVIZ-Setup-0.1.1.exe** below and run it (it updates 0.1.0 in place). The installer isn't code-signed yet: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New

- **Clips to the beat:** old newsreels, cartoons, classic TV, commercials or your own clips cut on the beat, with stutters, reverses, scratches and freezes at the ends of bars. 12 new looks in Live Action, Cartoon and Random Clips to BPM.
- **Your music videos:** put a video named like `Artist - Title.mp4` in the videos folder (Master tab, Open my videos folder) and the My Music Video looks play it in time with the song.
- **Perform tab:** Cue mode (prepare the next look in the preview while the screen keeps the current one, then GO on the next bar), blackout, strobe and freeze buttons, sets played in order, and **MIDI learn** for any controller: press Learn, then touch the pad or knob.
- **Lyrics files apply on their own:** `.lrc` files saved into the lyrics folder (by you or another program) show up straight away, matched by name or by the tags inside them.

## Better

- **Album covers** come straight from Windows as soon as a song starts, even when Spotify is slow or rate-limiting.
- **More songs get lyrics:** lyrics without timestamps are spread across the song, and songs with several artists are found more often.
- **No placeholder words:** looks show the sung line, else the song's name, else nothing at all.

## Good to know

- Spotify lyrics need a free Spotify developer app (see the README). Lyrics are copyrighted: showing them at a public or commercial event needs a licence from the rights holders.
- If your DJ software has your controller open and BOOFVIZ doesn't see it, Windows is letting one app use it at a time; an up-to-date Windows 11 shares it between apps.
- Ableton Link is not included in this build (the Link SDK is GPL-licensed).
