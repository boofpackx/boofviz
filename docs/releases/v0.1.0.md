The first Windows release of BOOFVIZ: a real-time audio visualizer and auto-VJ that listens to whatever is playing on the computer and drives beat-locked visuals on a second screen, projector or LED wall.

## Install

1. Download **BOOFVIZ-Setup-0.1.0.exe** below and run it.
2. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Start BOOFVIZ. The control window and the output window open together, and System Audio capture starts right away. Play a track and the visuals lock to it within a few seconds. Press **F** to fullscreen the output.

## What's in it

- **190 built-in looks** across equalizers, 2D and 3D, trippy, club, real 90s, Y2K, retro type, lost media, retro TV, brutalist, neo 90s and vintage.
- **Auto-VJ:** shuffle that plays every look fairly, beat-quantized changes, playlists (90s night, vintage, lyrics, chill, peak time and more).
- **Lyrics:** synced lyrics for whatever Spotify is playing, shown over any look or as music-video lyric looks; lyrics without timestamps are spread across the song. Follows the Spotify desktop app through the Windows media session, so it stays clear of Spotify's rate limit.
- **Lost media and retro TV:** real archive footage from before 2003, pulled from an online archive, with tape and film damage, an old-TV screen, and your own videos folder.
- **Build your own:** up to 8 layers, blend modes, masks and effects, modulation from the music, 8 macros per look, presets as plain JSON.
- **Audio from anywhere:** System Audio (no drivers), any input or audio interface, virtual cables, or a dropped audio file.

## Good to know

- **Spotify lyrics** need a free Spotify developer app (see the README). Lyrics are copyrighted: showing them at a public or commercial event needs a licence from the rights holders.
- **Ableton Link** is not included in this build (the Link SDK is GPL-licensed). Tap tempo, MIDI clock and the automatic beat tracker all work.
- Settings, your presets and lyrics live in `%APPDATA%\BOOFVIZ`.
