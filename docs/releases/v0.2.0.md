BOOFVIZ 0.2.0 puts lyrics on every look, your way, and adds four looks built around new ways of showing them.

## Install

Download **BOOFVIZ-Setup-0.2.0.exe** below and run it (it updates earlier versions in place). The installer isn't code-signed yet: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New

- **Lyrics mode: Off / Looks' own / Everywhere.** One switch (Lyrics tab, Perform tab, the **L** key or a MIDI button). In Everywhere a look that already shows lyrics stays exactly as it is, a look whose lyrics are switched off gets them switched on (including your favourites where you'd turned a lyric layer off), and any other look gets the lyric style for its theme. Off hides every lyric, the looks' own included. Your saved looks are never rewritten.
- **Lyric styles gallery.** Styles are grouped by family with a live sample of the line being sung: hover a card to try it in the preview (never on the screen), click to apply it to the look's theme, just this look, or all looks. Search, Surprise me, and Make your own style. A Themes table sets the style per theme; each look can be set to its theme's style, its own lyrics only, a style of its own, or never (also from the library's right-click menu). **Shift+L** steps through styles live. Library cards show each look's lyric state.
- **Four hero lyric looks:**
  - **Step Chart** (Pop Culture): the arcade dance game. Every sung word is a step note that lands on the arrows as it is sung, coloured by its rhythm, with freeze arrows for held words, PERFECT / GREAT, a combo and chorus fever. Single, Double and Reverse.
  - **Hot Metal** (Vintage): brass matrices drop into the composing stick word by word, then the line is cast and struck onto cotton paper, the hero word in wood type. Proof Press, Poster and Newsroom.
  - **Keyframes** (Y2K & Aero): the early-2000s web-animation tool. Words drop keyframes on the timeline and tween onto the stage with onion skins; choruses play the test movie, a cartoon singer mouthing every letter. Studio, Test Movie and Onion Skin.
  - **Poured** (Brutalist): every line cast in concrete, poured word by word from a crane, stripped of its formwork and stacked into a tower as the sky goes from dawn to night. Also Hard Sun, Night Shift and Blueprint.
- **Hero styles** for any look: Step lane, Printed strip and Tweening words, in the gallery's Hero family.
- **Bundled typefaces**, so text looks the same on every PC.

## Good to know

- Your old "Show lyrics over every look" setting carries over as Everywhere, and a style you'd picked there becomes a saved style for all looks.
- Lyrics are copyrighted: showing them at a public or commercial event needs a licence from the rights holders.
- Ableton Link is not included in this build (the Link SDK is GPL-licensed).
