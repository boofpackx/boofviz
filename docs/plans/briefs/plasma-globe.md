# Brief: Plasma Globe (the novelty lamp)

**Tells:** a dark glass sphere; a bright white-pink core electrode in the middle; 8-14 violet-pink lightning tendrils with white-hot cores crawling from the core to the glass, wriggling and forking near the glass; a bright spot where each tendril meets the glass; tendrils at the front reach the glass inside the disc, not only at the rim (it is 3D); a faint glass reflection and rim highlight.
**Anti-tells:** straight lines, rigid branches, blue electric arcs, a detailed lamp base.
**Music map:** kick: every tendril gathers to one touch point on the glass (like a fingertip), then lets go over a beat; hats: tendril flicker; bass: core brightness and tendril thickness; bar: the touch point moves; drop: the whole globe crackles (all tendrils fork, flash); breakdown: few slow tendrils; silence: dim idle tendrils.
**Variety:** tendril count and wander drift by phrase; colour from the palette.
