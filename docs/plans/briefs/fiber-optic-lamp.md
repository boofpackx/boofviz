# Brief: Fiber Optic Lamp (the novelty lamp)

**Tells:** a fountain of hundreds of fine fibres springing from a point at the bottom and arcing outwards; the fibres themselves barely visible, their tips bright points of light; the colour wheel in the base tinting the spray, turning through colours (sometimes two colours split across the spray); the tips sway together; a soft reflection pool below.
**Anti-tells:** thick lines, firework bursts, a detailed base.
**Music map:** beat: the colour wheel steps round a notch; bass: the spray breathes wider; hats: random tips sparkle; kick: tips flare; drop: a fast full colour spin; breakdown: slow sway, single colour; silence: the wheel stops.
**Variety:** spray shape (dome, fountain, fan) per phrase; one-, two- or rainbow-colour wheel.
