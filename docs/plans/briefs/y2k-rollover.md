# Brief: Y2K Rollover (New Year's Eve 1999)

**Tells**
1. A huge seven-segment countdown (HH:MM:SS style, then just seconds), red-orange LED glow on black, ghost segments visible.
2. The final ten seconds, each digit slamming in.
3. "1999" changing to "2000", and then the bug: the year flips to "1900", digits scramble.
4. Fireworks: rising trails, bursts of sparks falling with gravity, crackle glitter.
5. The blue full-screen fatal error page with white monospace text and the "press any key" line.
6. TV broadcast feel: slight interlace, a corner station bug (generic).

**Anti-tells:** modern smooth digits, confetti emoji, any real channel or OS name.

**Music map:** the countdown is the phrase: zero exactly on the phrase downbeat; final 10 seconds = last 10 beats or 2.5 bars scaled to tempo; kick after midnight: firework bursts; snare: crackle; drop: the bug (digits scramble, blue error page for a bar); breakdown: fireworks only, slow; silence: digits hold.

**Variety:** cycle of phases per phrase: countdown → midnight fireworks → bug → error page → reboot to countdown; error text from the lyrics; firework colours from the palette.
