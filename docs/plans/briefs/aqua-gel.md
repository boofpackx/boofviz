# Brief: Aqua Gel (the glossy gel interface material, 2000-2003)

**Tells:** translucent candy-coloured gel capsules (pill-shaped bars) standing in a row; a bright glossy highlight across the top third of each; a lighter inner glow at the bottom; slightly darker rims; the light pinstripes of the background visible through the gel, bent; a soft coloured shadow under each capsule; everything clean and bright.
**Anti-tells:** flat colours, dark backgrounds, chrome, harsh black outlines.
**Music map:** bands: capsule heights; kick: the capsules wobble like jelly (squash and stretch); hats: highlights glint; drop: the capsules pop into gel bubbles that float up, then grow back; breakdown: slow sway; silence: capsules settle low.
**Variety:** single colour, two-tone or rainbow row; capsule count; light or graphite stripes.
