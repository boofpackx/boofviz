# Brief: Console Boot Fog (the 2000 console start-up)

**Tells:** a deep, dark blue void thick with fog; translucent glowing towers (tall boxes) rising out of the murk at different heights, their edges brighter than their faces; tiny light motes drifting; a slow, smooth camera glide; a sense of scale and calm; a soft swell of light at the end.
**Anti-tells:** hard shadows, solid opaque blocks, saturated rainbow colours, a logo.
**Music map:** spectrum: each tower's height follows its band, rising slowly; bass: the fog glow breathes; kick: motes brighten; phrase: a soft white swell of light and the glide turns; drop: every tower surges up together; breakdown: towers sink, fog thickens; silence: still.
**Variety:** glide direction and height change each phrase; tower field layout shifts.
