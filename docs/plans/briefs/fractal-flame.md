# Brief: Fractal Flame (the 1999 distributed screensaver)

**Tells**
1. Silky, smoky filaments of light, like curling flames or silk ribbons, on black.
2. Log-density brightness: thin wisps are faint and soft, dense folds burn bright, almost white.
3. Colour flows along the structure (each fold has its own hue blend), not random.
4. Slow, mesmerising morph from one shape into the next.
5. Fine grain in the dark parts, no hard edges anywhere.
6. Often a symmetry (2-, 3- or 5-fold) or a spiral.

**Anti-tells:** solid shapes, hard outlines, flat colours, fast motion.

**Music map:** phrase: morph to a new flame; bass: breathes the transform weights; kick: brightness flare of the densest folds; build: rotation speeds up; drop: the flame shatters into sparks and re-forms over a bar; breakdown: slow morph, dimmer; silence: frozen and glowing.

**Variety:** a library of 10+ hand-picked flames, symmetry varied, palette-driven colour.
