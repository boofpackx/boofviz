# Brief: Synesthesia Rail (the 2001 trance rail shooter)

**Tells:** flying forward through a translucent glowing wireframe world; wireframe targets drifting in; thin corner brackets locking on to them; a beam from the centre to each locked target and a burst of line fragments when it is hit, always on the musical grid; the world changing form (grid ocean, tunnel, lattice, void) between sections; heavy bloom; cyan, magenta, white.
**Anti-tells:** solid shading, gore, a character model, off-grid timing.
**Music map:** lock-ons and hits come from the rhythm (the last bar's onsets, predicted one bar ahead on the 16th-note grid); kick: the world pulses; phrase: the world changes form; build: more targets, faster flight; drop: everything on screen bursts; breakdown: void with drifting particles; silence: slow drift.
**Variety:** four world forms, three target shapes, colour cycling per phrase.
