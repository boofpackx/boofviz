# Brief: Bullet Time (the 1999 frozen-moment shot)

**Tells**
1. Hundreds of glass shards and water droplets frozen mid-air, filling the space around the centre.
2. The camera swings round them in a smooth arc while they hang still.
3. The arc has a slight stutter, as if made of many still cameras.
4. A cool green tint and high contrast, deep blacks, bright speculars.
5. Ripples of displaced air (refractive rings) trailing a few objects.
6. When time runs, everything streaks with motion blur; frozen, everything is razor sharp.
7. Dust motes in a shaft of light.

**Anti-tells:** cartoony shapes, warm colours, soft focus while frozen, uniform spacing.

**Music map:** phrase downbeat: the burst (time runs fast for a beat, everything flies out); then freeze and orbit through the phrase; kick: the orbit steps (camera-array stutter); build: the orbit speeds up and time creeps; drop: time snaps back, shards fly past the camera, new burst; breakdown: slow drift, dust shaft; silence: complete freeze.

**Variety:** burst centre and shapes change each phrase; orbit direction and height alternate; mix of shards/droplets changes.
