# Brief: Silhouette Dance (the 2003 music-player ads)

**Tells**
1. One totally flat, saturated background colour filling the frame: lime, hot pink, electric blue, orange, yellow, purple.
2. A hard cut to the next colour (on the bar), never a fade.
3. Pure black dancer silhouettes, no interior detail at all, no outline, no shadow.
4. Silhouettes are full-body, strong poses: knees bent, elbows out, head bobbing, big airborne moments.
5. Thin pure white earphone cord running from the ears down to a hand or hip, the only non-black element, whipping with the motion.
6. A small white rectangle (the player) in a hand or at the hip, nothing on it.
7. Dancer usually centred or slightly off-centre, large, feet near the bottom third.
8. Sometimes two dancers, mirrored or offset; occasionally a close crop on the upper body.
9. No floor, no horizon, no texture.

**Anti-tells:** gradients, outlines, drop shadows, detailed hands or faces, neon glow, film grain, any text.

**Music map:** kick: the bounce (knees bend down on the beat); snare: arms snap to a new pose and the cord whips; hats: head bob; bass: body lean depth; bar: colour cut; phrase: new dance (move set) and sometimes a framing change (wide / close / two dancers); build: moves speed up to double time; drop: jump and freeze for one beat, then two dancers; breakdown: one dancer, slow sway, colour holds; silence: the dancer stops in a pose.

**Variety:** 8+ moves (bounce step, running man, arm wave, jump, spin, shoulder pump, lean back, head nod), two framings, one or two dancers, mirrored pairs, colour order from the palette.

**Risk gate:** a still and a 4-frame motion test first; if the poses don't read as natural dancing, drop it.
