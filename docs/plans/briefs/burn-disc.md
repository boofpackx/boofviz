# Brief: Burn Disc (the CD-R mix era, 1998-2005)

**Tells:** a recordable disc close up, filling the frame; the dye layer's green, blue or gold tint; a rainbow diffraction streak across the surface that moves as the light moves; fine concentric track grooves; the burned area darker than the unburned, with a visible edge; the clear plastic hub ring and the hole; small capacity text printed round the hub.
**Anti-tells:** a label with art, a brand name, a cartoon disc, a flat rainbow gradient.
**Music map:** tempo: the disc spins; kick: the diffraction flashes; each phrase burns another band of tracks outward; drop: the light swings right round the disc; breakdown: slow light; silence: the disc spins down.
**Variety:** dye colour from the palette; light path; burn progress through the song.
