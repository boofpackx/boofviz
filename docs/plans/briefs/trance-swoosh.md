# Brief: Trance Swoosh (2000s trance compilation art)

**Tells:** a deep blue-black background; a glowing white-cyan core; electric-blue light swooshes arcing round it on tilted orbits, tapered thin at the tail and bright at the head; a long horizontal anamorphic lens streak through the core; thin starburst rays; hairline orbit rings; a faint hexagon grid fading in and out; everything over-glowed.
**Anti-tells:** hard flat shapes, warm sunset palette (unless chosen), text.
**Music map:** kick: a new swoosh launches and sweeps for a bar; bass: the core pulses; snare: the lens streak flashes; phrase: the hex grid fades in, orbit tilts change; build: swooshes speed up; drop: a big starburst and every swoosh flares; breakdown: one slow swoosh; silence: core only.
**Variety:** up to eight swooshes at different tilts and radii, colour from the palette.
